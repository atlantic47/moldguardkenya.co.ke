## Why Does Mold Thrive in Kilimani?
- **Rapid Construction Flaws:** Sometimes, new buildings suffer from incomplete curing of concrete or poorly installed roofing and plumbing, leading to hidden moisture traps in walls and shafts.
- **Poor Ventilation in Apartments:** Many modern Kilimani apartments lack adequate cross-ventilation, leading to trapped humidity that feeds structural mold in bathrooms and bedrooms.
- **Balcony and Window Seepage:** Heavy rains often exploit weak seals around sliding doors and windows, allowing water to saturate interior drywall.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Mold in Kilimani
### My Kilimani apartment is new. Why is there already mould?
New concrete and plaster hold a lot of water, and many units are occupied before the building has dried out. Add windowless bathrooms and sealed aluminium windows, and the moisture has nowhere to go except onto the coldest wall.

### The mould is on the wall I share with a neighbour. What can I do?
Book an inspection first. If the moisture readings show the water is coming through from the other side, the report records it, and the repair has to happen at the source before treatment on your side will last.
