## Common Mold Triggers in Lavington
- **Complex Roof Structures:** Many large Lavington homes feature complex, multi-pitched roofs. Valleys and hidden gutters can easily block with leaves from mature trees, leading to water overflows into the ceiling and subsequent attic mold.
- **Rising Damp in Older Foundations:** Changes in the water table and degrading damp-proof courses in established homes frequently lead to peeling paint and mold on the lower third of interior walls.
- **En-Suite Bathroom Condensation:** Large, poorly ventilated master bathrooms trap massive amounts of steam, fueling aggressive mold growth on ceilings and high walls.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Mold in Lavington
### Why is there mould inside my built-in wardrobes?
Wardrobes fixed to an external wall trap still, cold air. Moisture condenses on the wall behind the back panel and mould spreads onto clothes and shoes. We treat the wall and advise on ventilation gaps so it does not return.

### My townhouse has a flat roof terrace. Could that be the cause?
Very often. Waterproofing membranes on terraces crack with sun and age, and water then tracks through the slab to the ceiling below. Our moisture mapping shows whether the damp pattern matches the terrace above.

### How long will the work take?
A single bathroom is often finished in a day. A bedroom that needs structural drying takes several days because the dehumidifiers must run until the readings are normal.
