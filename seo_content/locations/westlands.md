## Frequently Asked Questions About Mold in Westlands
### Can you treat an office without closing it?
Usually, yes. We can work in the evening or over a weekend and keep the area contained, so the rest of the floor stays in use. Tell us your working hours when you book.

### There is a musty smell from the air conditioning. Is that mould?
It often is. Condensate trays and ducts stay wet, and the fan then spreads spores through the room. We inspect the unit and the ceiling void before recommending cleaning.

### Why do homes in Spring Valley, Loresho and Kitisuru get so much mould?
These valleys are cooler, wetter and more shaded than the rest of the city, and many houses are built into slopes, so ground water presses against the lower walls.
