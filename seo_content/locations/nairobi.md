## How Our Mold (Mould) Removal Process Works in Nairobi
Whether you spell it mold or mould, the fix follows the same order, and skipping a step is why so many Nairobi walls go black again a few months after being repainted.
- **Inspection and moisture mapping:** We trace the damp to its source with moisture meters and thermal imaging, whether that is a leaking flat roof, a failed bathroom seal or condensation in a closed-up bedroom.
- **Containment:** The affected room is sealed with plastic sheeting so spores do not travel into the rest of the house or the neighbouring flat.
- **HEPA air scrubbing:** Filtration machines run throughout the job to capture airborne spores.
- **Removal and antimicrobial treatment:** Contaminated material that cannot be saved is removed, and the remaining surfaces are treated to kill the roots.
- **Drying and verification:** Dehumidifiers bring the structure back to a normal moisture level, and we confirm the result before we leave.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Mold in Nairobi
### When is mold worst in Nairobi?
Problems peak during and just after the long rains from March to May and the short rains from October to December. July and August bring a second wave: the weather is cold and overcast, windows stay shut, and condensation builds up on north-facing walls and behind wardrobes.

### Is mould in a Nairobi rental the tenant's or the landlord's responsibility?
It depends on the cause. Leaking roofs, plumbing failures and rising damp are building defects, while mould caused by drying laundry indoors or never opening windows is usually a ventilation issue. Our inspection report states the cause in writing, which helps both sides settle the question.

### Do you work in apartments as well as houses?
Yes. Much of our Nairobi work is in apartment blocks, where bathrooms without windows and shared plumbing shafts are the most common sources of damp.
