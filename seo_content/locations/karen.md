## How Our Mold (Mould) Removal Process Works in Karen
Large Karen homes need a slightly different approach from a city apartment, because the damp usually comes from the ground or the roof space instead of a single bathroom.
- **Whole-property moisture survey:** We check external walls, the roof void, basements and staff quarters, since damp on a big plot is rarely limited to one room.
- **Source repair advice:** Blocked gutters under overhanging trees, failed damp-proof courses and borehole plumbing leaks are identified and documented for your contractor.
- **Containment and HEPA filtration:** The affected rooms are sealed and the air is filtered so spores do not settle in the rest of the house.
- **Treatment and removal:** Mouldy ceiling boards, skirting and timber that cannot be saved are removed, and the remaining structure is treated.
- **Structural drying:** Commercial dehumidifiers run until walls and floors return to a normal moisture reading.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Mold in Karen
### Why does mould keep returning on my Karen home's outer walls?
Shade is the usual reason. Mature trees keep walls from drying after rain, and soil or flower beds banked against the wall bridge the damp-proof course. Unless the wall is allowed to dry and the moisture path is cut, repainting only hides the mould for a season.

### Can you treat a guest cottage or staff quarters at the same visit?
Yes. Outbuildings are often the worst affected because they are heated and ventilated less than the main house. We inspect them during the same site visit.

### Is the treatment safe for horses, dogs and garden plants?
We use low-toxicity antimicrobial products indoors and keep treated rooms closed until they are dry. Tell the technician about animals on the property so they can plan the work around them.
